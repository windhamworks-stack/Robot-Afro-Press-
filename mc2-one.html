MC2 ONE

They tried to silence him… they only made his voice stronger.

The Sound of Resistance

MC2 One is a young underground artist with a voice powerful enough to move a city—and abilities capable of transforming sound itself into a force for justice.

After one final open-mic performance turns him into a street legend, MC2 attracts the attention of Titan Vale, the ruthless owner of Vale House Records. Vale offers him five million dollars for his voice, his future and his freedom. MC2 refuses.

Vale responds with violence. MC2 is attacked and left for dead, while his friend Echo Rae loses an arm at Vale’s hands. But MC2 returns from the grave stronger than before—and Echo Rae returns with a powerful bionic arm built by the mysterious Mr. Junkman.

Summer-Bam

During the massive Summer-Bam concert, Titan Vale publicly mourns the artist he believes he destroyed. Then MC2 One appears in the sky.

Before a live audience, MC2 hijacks the Jumbotron and exposes Vale’s criminal empire as federal agents raid Vale House Records. MC2 defeats him without throwing a punch, proving that truth—when amplified—can be more powerful than violence.

Echo Rae

DJ, podcaster, writer and fearless investigator, Echo Rae is more than MC2 One’s partner. She is the person who collected the evidence that brought Titan Vale’s empire crashing down.

MC2 One controls sound. Echo Rae makes certain the truth is heard.
