About This Project

Mousetronautz Issue #1

My love of comic books began during my childhood in Delaware. From that point on, I dreamed of someday writing a comic book of my own.

Mousetronautz was an accident. After one of my superhero scripts was rejected, I found myself doodling a mouse. My sister named the mouse in our house Jackson, and that name stayed with me. From that simple drawing, Captain Jackson and the world of Mousetronautz began to take shape.

What started as one idea continued to grow until it became a story planned across twelve issues.

Finding the right artist to bring that world to life was another journey. Eventually, I connected with Vera Kulikov, also known as Wednesday 7. Through our collaboration—and the technology available to us—we were able to turn the idea into the comic you have just read.

I want to thank my father, my mother, and everyone who supported and encouraged me throughout this journey.

The future of the remaining eleven issues is still uncertain. Perhaps one day, with enough interest and support, a Kickstarter campaign can help complete the full story.
