Welcome to the Other Side of Imagination.

We don’t just create superheroes. We build histories, cultures, and legends.

Robot Afro Press characters and concepts began with original hand-drawn artwork, writing, and ideas developed over many years. AI is used as a creative development and reference tool to explore visual directions and refine concepts. As Robot Afro Press grows, we intend to work with artists to bring these stories to life. Robot Afro Press is not an AI brand—it is an independent creative universe built from human imagination.
