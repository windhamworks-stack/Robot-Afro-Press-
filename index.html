Comics. Characters. Worlds. Stories.

We don’t just create superheroes. We create worlds, histories, cultures, and legends. Welcome to the other side of imagination.

Robot Afro Press characters and concepts began with original hand-drawn artwork, writing, and ideas developed over many years. AI is used as a creative development and reference tool to help explore and refine those ideas. As Robot Afro Press grows, we intend to work with artists to bring these stories to life. Robot Afro Press is not an AI brand—it is an independent creative universe built from human imagination.
