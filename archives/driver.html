DRIVER

THE HAMMER OF OGUN

Before Joan Henry wielded it, Ogun held it in his own hand.

Driver is the personal forge hammer of the Orisha of iron, tools, technology, labor, and war. How it came to rest inside an ancient chest in Joan’s grandmother’s basement remains a mystery. But when a sickly young girl entered that basement, the chest burst open—and the hammer flew into her hand.

It was barely larger than a craftsman’s mallet.

As Joan grew, Driver grew with her. She first used it to transform discarded metal into extraordinary sculptures, forging shapes and alloys that defied conventional science. Only later would the artist discover what her tool could do in battle.

Through Driver, Joan commands gravity—making crushing weight nearly weightless, pinning enemies to the ground, taking flight, and concentrating devastating force into a single strike. The lights swirling within its massive head resemble distant galaxies, but they are something far older: sparks of divine forge-fire.

The Children of Ogun have searched generations for their sacred artifact. Some demand its return. Yet the hammer answers Joan’s call.

Ogun forged weapons of war with it. Joan Henry forges hope.
