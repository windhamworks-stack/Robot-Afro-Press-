BUM-RUSH

The Fastest Man Nowhere

Bum-Rush is a man out of place. Beneath a weathered hoodie and worn trench coat, he carries a past he rarely speaks about. Whatever happened to him has left him nearly broken, drifting through a world where he has yet to find a sense of belonging.

Most days, he wants to be left alone—to drown his sorrows and forget. But the present keeps intruding. There is always another problem, another person in danger, or another familiar voice insisting that he get up and do something.

Bum-Rush possesses extraordinary speed, but rarely reveals the extent of his abilities. Something holds him back—a fear that surfaces whenever he pushes himself too far. Questions about where he came from, what he lost, and why he stopped running usually go unanswered.

His closest friend, Insector-X, knows more than most. He also refuses to leave him alone. The two have ties to both 1331 and Shadow Company, though neither is a formal member of either organization. Those connections—and X’s persistence—keep drawing Bum-Rush into situations that demand the very thing he has tried to abandon: being a hero.

Beneath his battered outer layers, a blue costume still bears an infinity-shaped lightning emblem. He has kept it through everything, even as he tries to leave the man who wore it behind.

Bum-Rush wants the world to let him disappear. But trouble keeps finding him—and when it does, he still has to decide whether to walk away or run toward it.
