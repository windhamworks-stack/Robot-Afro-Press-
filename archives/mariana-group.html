THE RAP ARCHIVES

THE MARIANA GROUP

Director Alaric Renner moves comfortably among the powerful while quietly pursuing power of his own. Believed by some to be immortal, he possesses super-soldier physical abilities and has studied nearly every fighting style on Earth. His crystal-skull cane makes him even more dangerous—sensing hostile intent, creating localized time bubbles, projecting illusions, influencing minds, and shattering objects through resonance.

Renner heads the Mariana Group, a respected institution whose marine conservation, scientific research, and deep-sea exploration produce genuine benefits. Its government contracts, wealthy donors, and institutional partnerships also provide the resources and access he needs to pursue extraordinary relics. Most of its staff remain unaware of the ambitions concealed within their work.

Behind the scenes, Renner competes with other agencies such as 1331 and M.O.D.U., sometimes manipulating their operations to advance his own plans. Beside him stands Nest, his trusted operative and bodyguard, capable of changing size and manifesting multiple bodies at once. Proximity to Renner’s cane quiets his fractured mind, giving him focus and purpose—and a deeply personal reason to protect the man who carries it.

In a world of monsters and Metanaturals, some threats announce themselves. Others shake your hand.
