THE RAIL SPLITTER

THE AMERICAN EXCALIBUR

Before it hung above a fireplace, broken and rusting, the Rail Splitter helped save the world.

Its haft was fashioned from the wood of the First Tree, sealed beneath impenetrable, amber-like sap. Its silver blade, edged in red, was forged from meteorite metal where it fell to Earth. Its history connects Dogon mystics, Abraham Lincoln, and the man now called Old Skool.

At its heart lies the power of truth. Only Cain, the first murderer and avatar of death, possesses a soul wicked enough to turn it.

When Old Skool fought as Buffalo Souljah, he stood with his allies against Cain. Victory cost him part of his immortality and drained the Rail Splitter’s power. He hung the ruined axe above his fireplace—a reminder of what saving the world had cost.

Years later, his grandson accidentally knocked it down while exploring the study. When the boy picked it up, his body glowed. Splintered wood mended. Rusted metal became whole.

His Ka energy had awakened, revealing its defining gift: he could make things better.

The Rail Splitter passed to its new wielder—Buffalo Warrior. But Old Skool knew a weapon with that history does not awaken again without a reason.

Somewhere, a reckoning was waiting.
