OPTIKA

Real Name: Tatianna Thompson

“The last thing you’ll ever see.”

Tatianna Thompson was watching her world disappear. Faces, colors, familiar places—everything was slowly slipping out of focus. Her mother was determined to keep her daughter from losing it all.

A neural-repair specialist at Bionuvex, her mother was developing a process called Neuralattice Infusion, using injectable microscopic machines to repair damaged neural tissue and restore sight. When government funding was redirected toward more promising research, she refused to abandon the project. She continued in secret until Bionuvex discovered her unauthorized work and fired her.

With her daughter’s sight fading and her access to the laboratory gone, she stole the technology and administered the treatment herself.

Tatianna opened her eyes. The world came back.

Then she began seeing things no one else could.

The nanites had repaired her visual pathways, but they continued adapting, augmenting her brain’s visual-processing center far beyond their intended purpose. Walls became transparent. Darkness revealed its secrets. Energy shimmered through people and machines. Even time offered glimpses of what had happened—and what might happen next.

Then her gaze began to burn.

Now known as Optika, Tatianna possesses an arsenal behind her eyes. She can follow a bullet in flight, unravel an illusion, or bend another person’s will through direct eye contact. Her eyes can unleash searing heat, precision cutting beams, and concussive blasts capable of smashing through targets.

Her most devastating attack, Spectrum Overload, occurs when she forces her normally separate energy channels open at once. The resulting feedback surge erupts through her eyes in a white-hot blast threaded with rainbow energy—burning, cutting, and striking with tremendous force.

But every ability brings a shift in temperament. One makes her calculating. Another feeds her suspicions. Others awaken a hunger for control. Her destructive powers bring out the side of Tatianna that enjoys watching people flinch—and that side increasingly calls the shots. The more she indulges it, the stronger its hold becomes.

Her mother wanted her to see the world again.

Optika intends to make sure the world sees her.
