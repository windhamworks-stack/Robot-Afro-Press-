THE RAP ARCHIVES

SHADOW COMPANY

Before the modern age of Metanaturals, there was Buffalo Souljah.

The first hero is believed to have died long ago, as his grandson, Buffalo Warrior, carries his legacy forward. Only a select few know the truth: the legend Buffalo Souljah lives on as Old Skool.

Today, he stands at the heart of Shadow Company—a family whose reach extends across generations, whose resources run deep, and whose full history remains out of public view.

In plain sight stands Bionuvex: a technology and biotechnology company with government and military contracts. Its customers buy advanced breakthroughs; however, they have never seen its best work. Old Skool’s public role as Operations Manager gives little indication of his true position—or the power he wields.

Old Skool’s family ties run deeper. His daughter, Agent Tubman, is an archaeologist and tomb raider who wields the Staff of Moses. Her husband, Turner, brings a genius-level mind to the clandestine work taking place at Bionuvex.

Within those walls, Turner brought D.U.D.E. to life—a bioengineered being combining Grey alien biology with human stem cells. As D.U.D.E. developed, he became a sentient individual with his own mind, his own choices, and a place in the family.

Turner’s bioengineered mask, originally developed to aid in D.U.D.E.’s creation, has since evolved far beyond its original purpose.

MODU keeps track of the world’s Metanaturals. Old Skool intends to keep a few off the books.
