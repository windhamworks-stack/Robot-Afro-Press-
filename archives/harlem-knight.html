HARLEM KNIGHT

THE WILL OF THE STREETS

“Harlem 1st, the world 2nd.”

Before Harlem Knight became a protector of his neighborhood, Lennox was a gifted young baseball player learning how to survive. By thirteen, he could fight grown men in underground circuits. Baseball was his passion, but fighting—and later bodyguard work—helped pay the bills. Somewhere between the diamond and the streets, he found a purpose bigger than himself.

Harlem Knight possesses tremendous strength and near-invulnerability, but his will makes him especially dangerous. He can condense the atoms in objects he touches, making an ordinary baseball bat as resistant to destruction as he is. He can leap from the street to a tenement rooftop, then momentarily solidify the air beneath his feet to change direction in midair. Some believe the respect he commands makes him even more powerful.

As a member of Shadow Company’s second cell, Lennox brings more than muscle. His network of trusted lawyers, judges, prison guards, and social workers helps steer vulnerable young people toward a second chance. He knows he cannot save everyone, but he fights to reach those he can. And when larger missions call him away, Shadow Company sends operatives to protect the streets in his place. For Harlem Knight, protecting the neighborhood means showing up—and making sure someone still does when he cannot.

Protecting Harlem isn’t just what he does. It’s who he is.
