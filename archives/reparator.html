THE REPARATOR

SEEK JUSTICE—NEVER REVENGE

Before it rested behind museum glass, the Reparator stood against an invasion.

Shaped in the image of an African pick, its twin blades were wielded by a defender who brought down thousands of colonizers before falling. The invaders seized the weapon and divided it—one blade taken to Britain, the other to Portugal.

But possession did not mean ownership.

When Sheba touched the blade in a British museum, visions of its violent history overwhelmed her. Beyond those visions waited an entity called Justice—and an offer that would change her life.

She accepted, becoming its avatar: Sheba — The Superbad.

Wielded as twin swords or united into one formidable blade, the Reparator can free the just from any bind and deliver more devastating blows to the unrighteous. It amplifies Sheba’s ability to sense good and evil, and produces temporary, smaller versions of itself that she uses as throwing weapons. Its strength is sufficient to withstand even the might of Excalibur.

In the hands of a master swordswoman who pursues predators and the powerful interests protecting them, its unfinished history finds a new bearer.

Her charge is clear: recover what was taken, reunite what was divided, and seek justice—never revenge.

They displayed it as a trophy. Sheba carries it as a promise.
