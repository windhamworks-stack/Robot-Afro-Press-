SYNERGY

THE WORKING MAN’S VILLAIN

Before he became Synergy, Ellis Mercer worked as a janitor at a research facility housing an experimental supercollider. While others studied the mysteries of existence, he cleaned the floors beneath their feet. When attending his best friend’s funeral cost him his job, grief became anger. He returned to sabotage the machine—and the resulting catastrophe transformed him.

Ellis emerged with the extraordinary ability to convert dark matter into cosmic energy. The facility was destroyed, but his anger survived. To him, the wreckage represented only one part of a much larger problem: institutions willing to spend billions reaching beyond Earth while denying ordinary workers the smallest measure of dignity.

Now Synergy follows the money, research, and corporate connections linking laboratories, executives, and defense contractors. Those pursuing exotic science or off-world technology while treating workers as disposable become targets. Even a company as powerful as Bionuvex can find itself in his sights.

Whether the accident altered his mind or power hardened his convictions, Ellis increasingly sees affiliation as guilt. He claims to fight for working people, yet his attacks threaten the very livelihoods and lives he says he wants to protect. Some see a man finally forcing the powerful to answer. Others see a catastrophe deciding who deserves to survive.

Synergy sees himself as the consequence of a world that refused to listen.

They denied him a day to grieve. Now he decides what their tomorrow looks like.
