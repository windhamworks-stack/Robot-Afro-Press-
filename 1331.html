1331

THE WEIRD THINGS AGENCY

UAPs. Extraterrestrials. Cryptids. Dimensional disturbances. Ancient artifacts. If something exists beyond conventional explanation, 1331 investigates it.

A super-secret paranormal intelligence agency, 1331 operates across borders with extraordinary autonomy and answers to very few authorities. Its agents investigate, contain, protect, negotiate with—or, when necessary, neutralize—the forces the public was never meant to know exist.

The American Division is led by Co-Directors Rascal and I-Lotus: a literal Bigfoot and a living artificial intelligence. Their methods are unconventional, their resources are nearly unmatched, and their authority is rarely questioned.

When 1331 arrives, the normal rules have already failed.

1331: UK DIVISION

DIRECTOR VONDA FROST

Although Vonda Frost appears to be a woman in her sixties, her actual age is unknown, and her history reaches into some of Britain’s most closely guarded secrets.

A veteran spy, Frost is connected to Britain’s earliest super-soldier program. The nature of that connection—and how far back her service truly goes—remains concealed.

Cynical, sharp-tongued, and composed under pressure, Frost brings a lifetime of experience to 1331’s UK Division. She holds the title of Director, but she and Agent Arthur share responsibility equally, facing Europe’s paranormal threats as a team. Her loyalty is to the cause, not necessarily the Crown.

Her two primary weapons reflect the strange territory she operates in: Mercy’s Thorn, a twisted magic wand capable of firing concentrated bursts of fairy dust, and an alien ray gun dating back to the 1950s. She rarely needs to settle matters with her fists, but beneath the trench coat is a woman more than capable of doing so.

Frost also personally oversees and expands the Dreadkeeping, 1331’s top-secret repository for objects too dangerous to destroy or release. She rarely opens it. When she does, the threat is extraordinary.

With a cigarette close at hand and an answer that usually reveals less than she knows, Vonda Frost guards the agency’s secrets as carefully as her own.

AGENT ARTHUR

Born to a king of King Arthur’s direct bloodline and a Scottish maid, Arthur’s life began with a secret. His mother was sent away to conceal the pregnancy, leaving the royal child to grow up far from the privilege of his father’s world.

His upbringing was modest, at times marked by poverty. School was a struggle, and fighting came early. Boxing and street fighting became the foundation of a combat style he would sharpen through mastery of various martial arts. Long before he carried Excalibur, Arthur knew how to hold his own.

Today, he wields the legendary sword and bears the Knight’s Fist, an ancient magical gauntlet that amplifies his strength, delivers devastating punches, and projects a protective force shield. When circumstances demand a final measure, it can unleash dragon’s breath.

Arthur serves alongside Director Vonda Frost in 1331’s UK Division. Frost holds the director’s title, but the two share responsibility equally, facing Europe’s paranormal threats as a team. Their work brings them into contact with ancient beings, powerful relics, and agreements between the human world and those who live beyond its sight.

How Arthur came to 1331 remains a mystery. So does the whereabouts of Merlin. For now, the man carrying Excalibur has work to do—and a legacy whose full story has yet to be told.
