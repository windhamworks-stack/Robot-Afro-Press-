IKENNA: SON OF ZEUS

Two worlds. Two legacies. One young hero caught between them.

Six completed comic-book scripts

Eric was an ordinary teenager trying to find his way forward after life knocked him down.

Then the impossible happened.

A mysterious encounter awakens extraordinary abilities within him and connects Eric to a hidden world of gods, monsters, ancient technology, and secrets stretching far beyond Earth.

Now calling himself Ikenna, Eric must learn to control powers he barely understands while searching for the truth about where they came from—and what his awakening may have set into motion.

Fortunately, he won’t face it alone. With his best friend M.K. at his side and the unpredictable Asterope guiding him, Ikenna is about to discover that the legends humanity calls mythology are very real.

And they know his name.

The age of heroes never ended. It was waiting for Ikenna.
