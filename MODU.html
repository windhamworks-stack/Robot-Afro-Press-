M.O.D.U.

METANATURAL OBSERVATION AND DETECTION UNIT

Meta-naturals are no longer isolated anomalies—they are a global reality. When superhuman activity threatens to overwhelm conventional authorities, M.O.D.U. responds.

The Metanatural Observation and Detection Unit is a government organization charged with monitoring, regulating, and policing the world’s growing meta-natural population. From unstable power events and unauthorized activity to threats ordinary law enforcement cannot contain, M.O.D.U. determines when to investigate, intervene, recruit, detain—or deploy force.

Operating from bases around the world, M.O.D.U. commands specialized strike teams equipped with advanced surveillance systems, containment technology, and weapons engineered specifically for meta-natural encounters. Its reach is global, its authority is extensive, and its files include heroes, villains, and unidentified subjects alike.

First appearing in Dazzle, M.O.D.U. stands between humanity and the superhuman age.
