KING FERATU

SOVEREIGN OF THE VAMPIRE NATION

When Vlad disappeared, he left behind an empty throne and a fractured Vampire Nation. King Feratu stepped into the void—but claiming power meant surviving those who wanted it for themselves.

For years, Feratu united the clans through alliances, submission, and war. Those who opposed his rule faced a relentless campaign until, at last, he emerged victorious: the true heir to the empty throne.

Vlad’s own son lent legitimacy to Feratu’s reign. Unready to claim his father’s mantle, he recognized the new king—with one condition: his own power would remain intact.

Yet Feratu’s victory never secured every clan’s loyalty. Those who refused to accept his reign retreated underground, becoming a cult devoted to Vlad’s return.

Meanwhile, the world changed. The rise of Metanaturals forced vampires to reckon with humans who could challenge—and destroy—them. Under Feratu, the Vampire Nation made treaties, cut deals, and adapted. Every agreement helped secure its place in this dangerous new age, while its king pursued a greater ambition: dominion over humanity.

Now, something is stirring.

Across the world, vampires feel an awakening. An ancient presence. The impossible suggestion that Vlad has returned.

Desperate to uncover the truth, Feratu sends vampires of every stripe and nation to find the missing ruler. His hard-won throne, carefully negotiated alliances, and plans for human domination could all unravel if Vlad steps out of the shadows.

Feratu spent years teaching the Vampire Nation to recognize its new king.

Now he must find Vlad before they remember their old one.
